# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

CloudFlared serves Indonesian website owners who want hosting that feels fast, safe, and easy to trust: small businesses, online shops, bloggers, agencies, and developers launching client sites. They usually arrive while comparing hosting providers, checking price, uptime, SSL, support, and whether the service looks credible enough for their business.

## Product Purpose

CloudFlared is a web hosting landing page that converts visitors into hosting customers. Success means the first screen communicates speed, security, reliability, and clear pricing without making users work to understand the offer. The site should make "start now" feel low-risk through uptime claims, SSL, support, pricing clarity, and money-back reassurance.

## Brand Personality

Fast, protected, and practical. The brand should feel like clean infrastructure made friendly: bright orange energy, crisp navy confidence, soft white technical surfaces, and direct Indonesian copy. It should feel modern and premium, but not cold or corporate.

## Anti-references

Do not make the site look like a generic purple SaaS template, a dark hacker dashboard, a cluttered hosting comparison page, or a stock-photo corporate site. Avoid excessive gradients, decorative blobs, unreadable tiny UI text, nested cards, mascot-first design, and gimmicky motion. The design should not feel like it was assembled from random landing page components.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with trust before detail: speed, security, uptime, SSL, and support must be visible before deep feature copy.
2. Make infrastructure feel tangible: use server, cloud, shield, lock, and network imagery as concrete product signals.
3. Keep conversion paths obvious: primary CTA should be unmistakable, with secondary package browsing nearby.
4. Use orange as energy, not noise: orange should guide action and brand memory, while navy carries confidence.
5. Stay scan-friendly: Indonesian copy should be short, direct, and easy to compare on desktop and mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Preserve keyboard focus states, reduced-motion behavior, and clear touch targets. Avoid relying on color alone for meaning. Keep body copy readable on mobile, and ensure pricing and CTAs remain usable at narrow widths.
